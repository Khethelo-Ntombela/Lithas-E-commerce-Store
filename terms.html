Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to Litha's Store. By accessing our platform, registering an account, or purchasing goods, you agree to comply with the following operational terms and conditions regulated under South African commercial law.

1. Terms of Use

Users must provide completely authentic registration details, including valid contact metrics and matching ID records. Any account found using fraudulent profiles or misrepresenting livestock and produce listings will face immediate termination from our marketplace networks.

2. The Buying Process

Purchasing goods on our marketplace follows a secure multi-step verification track to guarantee consumer safety:

  1. Selection & Checkout: The buyer populates their shopping cart and submits a payment through our secure PayFast payment gateway loop.
  2. Escrow Protection: Funds paid by the buyer are not delivered directly to the seller. Instead, the marketplace system automatically locks the payment balance in a secure administration escrow hold to satisfy the Consumer Protection Act (CPA).
  3. Order Confirmation: The buyer holds responsibility for navigating to their "My Orders" panel and clicking "Confirm Delivery Received" the moment the products arrive safely.

3. The Selling Process & Payouts

Registered vendors listing produce or livestock must follow our clear payout disbursement rules:

  1. Product Fulfillment: Sellers receive order alerts containing logistics specifications and must arrange immediate product distribution to the buyer.
  2. Balance Release: Payout rows will only generate and light up inside the Admin Panel after the buyer officially checks off on the delivery log.
  3. Platform Commission: To sustain market administrative overheads, a fixed 10% marketplace commission tracking split is automatically calculated and deducted from the gross sale price during manual electronic fund transfer (EFT) disbursements.

4. Limitation of Liability

Litha's Store serves as a secure clearing channel and marketplace engine. While we protect transactions via structured escrow protections, all users acknowledge that individual product delivery states, freight arrangements, and live animal grading checks remain a primary operational agreement settled directly between the respective buyer and seller.
