Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At Litha's Store, we take your personal data security very seriously. This policy outlines how we handle, collect, and protect your data in strict compliance with South African regulatory laws.

1. POPIA Compliance

In accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa, we only collect personal information that is absolutely necessary to create your account and complete transactions safely on our platform.

2. Information We Collect

When you register and use our storefront, we collect the following data parameters:

  • Your Full Name and Surname
  • Contact Details (Email Address and Phone Number)
  • South African 13-digit ID Number (for account security verification)
  • Date of Birth

3. How We Use Your Data

Your private profile parameters are used strictly for internal logistics and account validation. We use this data to verify buyers and sellers, process secure order matching records, and execute payout allocations. Your data will never be traded, shared, or exposed to third-party advertising networks.

4. Data Storage and Security

All gathered information is securely maintained inside closed, encrypted relational database instances. Profiles can be modified or checked at any time by raising a tracking support ticket request through our customer service center desk.
